# Fahim Bin Hasan

**History graduate and researcher in labor history, labor migration, and the history of technology in Bengal and Bangladesh.**

I hold a BA (Honours) and an MA in History from Dhaka College, University of Dhaka. I work with archival and documentary sources and qualitative methods to study how labor recruitment and training have shaped mobility, from colonial recruitment for the Assam tea gardens to today’s online freelancer training.

🌐 **Website:** [fahimbinhasan.github.io](https://fahimbinhasan.github.io)

## Research

- **Hasan, F. B. (2026).** *Selling the pipeline: Recruitment, training, and the sale of mobility in Bengal and Bangladesh, 1860s–2026* [Preprint]. SSRN. https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=7754499
- **Hasan, N. B., & Hasan, F. B. (2026).** *Who gets blamed for the blackout? A critical incident analysis of clients, reputation systems, and online freelancers* [Preprint]. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.28365

**Interests:** labor history · labor migration · history of technology · modern South Asia · qualitative and archival methods
